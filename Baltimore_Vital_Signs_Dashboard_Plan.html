Filters & actions

  • Global filter — Community (CSA) — applies to every worksheet on the dashboard.
  • "Recommended 5 only" parameter toggle — swaps both maps and the heatmap between full-city view and the five-community view without changing sheets.
  • Highlight action — clicking a community on either map highlights its row in the persistence heatmap and its card in the scorecard, and vice versa.
  • Navigation button — "See what's driving this" links to Dashboard 2.
